## Module 3 Resources

### Transformers
- Deep Learning Specialization — Sequence Models (Ng). RNNs, GRUs, LSTMs foundations motivating attention mechanisms. [Link](https://www.coursera.org/learn/nlp-sequence-models)
- Natural Language Processing Specialization — Attention Models. Seq2seq with attention, alignment intuition, and applications. [Link](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/natural-language-processing)
- Generative AI with Large Language Models (DeepLearning.AI & AWS). High-level transformer concepts, pretraining, fine-tuning, and evaluation. [Link](https://www.coursera.org/learn/generative-ai-with-llms)

### Attention fundamentals and visualizations
- The Illustrated Transformer (Jay Alammar). Clear visuals for self-attention, multi-head mechanisms, and embeddings. [Link](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/)
- Attention? Attention! (Lilian Weng). Conceptual tour of attention variants and design trade-offs. [Link](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-06-24-attention/)
- Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al.). Foundational paper introducing scaled dot-product self-attention. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) (academic)

### Encoder blocks
- Hugging Face Course — The Transformer. Encoder stack overview, layer roles, and intuition. [Link](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1)
- Transformers from Scratch (Peter Bloem). Builds encoder intuition with clear math and diagrams. [Link](https://peterbloem.nl/blog/transformers)
- Dive into Deep Learning — Transformer. Explains encoder components, normalization, and position encodings. [Link](https://d2l.ai/chapter_attention-mechanisms-and-transformers/index.html)

### Encoder–decoder architecture
- Hugging Face — Encoder-Decoder Overview — High-level guide to seq2seq models and usage patterns. [Link](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/model_doc/encoder-decoder)
- Dive into Deep Learning — Encoder–Decoder Models — Explains recurrent and transformer-based encoder–decoder frameworks step-by-step. [Link](https://d2l.ai/chapter_recurrent-modern/encoder-decoder.html)
